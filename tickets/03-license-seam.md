# Ticket 03: choose standard vs product (license seam)

**Status:** open
**Area:** business
**Source:** `docs/business-and-ip.md` section 8

## Why

The license seam depends on whether AISL is sold as a standard or a product.
That decision sets what stays open and what is paid, and it gates the packaging
and pricing work that follows.

- [ ] Read `docs/business-and-ip.md` section 8.
- [ ] Record the decision in `BRAND.md` or `DECISIONS.md`, including the
      consequence for the license seam.
- [ ] Update `docs/RELEASE.md` if the decision changes packaging.
